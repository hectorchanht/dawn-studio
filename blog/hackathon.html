How I rebuilt RealUFO in 6 days with Claude

我點樣用 Claude 6 日重建 RealUFO

For about two months, I didn’t touch RealUFO. The archive existed — hundreds of declassified records, sitting there — and I had no idea what it was supposed to become. So I left it alone.

Then a stranger posted in the /board/uap discussion board. Someone I’d never met cared enough about this weird little archive to write in it. That was the whole spark. I’ve been building intensely ever since.

The numbers

The repo has 868 commits over 76 days — and 797 of them, 92%, landed in six days. The peak was October 3rd: 327 commits in a single day. Not ingest churn — real features, reviewed and shipped.

My working pair through all of it: Claude. I’d describe what I wanted, it would build, I’d test, we’d iterate. Some nights we didn’t stop.

What got built

In that week, realufo.org went from a quiet archive to a living site: full-text search with page-level citations, AI summaries for the records, discussion boards, a government release tracker, a RAG “ask the archive” Q&A over a hundred thousand embedded chunks, email alerts for new releases, SEO and Google indexing, and the deploy pipeline fixed properly.

Every one of those was a commit. Three days had over a hundred.

The moment

Some nights I open a UFO video and go frame by frame, hunting for the thing that crosses the screen in a fraction of a second. Then I see it — a shape, gone in two frames, moving too fast for any eye to catch in real time.

That is the moment I see what my work is worth. I built the archive that holds that footage. Searchable, watchable, real. I did something useful — and I enjoy every second of it.

I don't know if what I saw is a UFO. But I know someone, someday, will sit down with these records and go frame by frame too. The archive will be there, because I built it.

What I learned

Ship daily or stall. The two months of silence taught me more than the sprint did — a project with no momentum is a project you’ve already quit. Daily deploys kept the loop tight: build, see it live, fix, repeat.

Let a real user pull you forward.

I didn’t restart because of a roadmap. I restarted because one person showed up.

Build the thing, put it where someone can find it, and listen when they knock.

AI pair programming is a force multiplier, not a replacement. I stayed the product mind — every decision, every “does this actually work,” every call on what matters. Claude stayed the hands. The judgment never left the room, because the judgment was mine.

I’ve since handed the ongoing care of realufo.org to Muse — monitoring, maintenance, the quiet work. I’m back to building. That’s what I do: I build things I use, and everyone to use.

差唔多兩個月，我冇掂過 RealUFO。個檔案庫喺度 — 幾百條解密紀錄，擺喺嗰度 — 而我唔知佢應該變成啲咩。所以我由佢。

跟住有個陌生人喺 /board/uap 討論區留咗言。一個我從未見過嘅人，居然在乎呢個古怪嘅細檔案庫，仲寫咗嘢入去。嗰下就係成個火花。自此我就冇停過咁起嘢。

數字

個 repo 76 日有 868 個 commits — 其中 797 個、92%，喺六日入面落地。最高峰係 10 月 3 號：單日 327 個 commits。唔係 ingest 嚟嚟去去嗰啲 — 係真 features，review 過、出咗貨嘅。

全程同我拍住上嘅係 Claude。我講我想點，佢起，我 test，我哋迭代。有啲夜晚，我哋冇停過。

起咗啲咩

嗰個星期，realufo.org 由一個安靜嘅檔案庫變成一個活生生嘅網站：全文搜尋連 頁級引用、紀錄嘅 AI 摘要、討論區、政府 發布追蹤器、一個喺十幾萬個 embedded chunks 上面行嘅 RAG 「問檔案庫」問答、新發布電郵提示、SEO 同 Google 索引，仲有 deploy pipeline 畀我正正經經修好。

嗰啲全部都係 commits。三日過百。

嗰一刻

有啲夜晚我開條 UFO 片，逐格逐格睇，揾嗰樣喺一秒都唔夠入面掠過畫面嘅嘢。跟住我見到喇 — 一個形狀，兩格就冇咗，快到真實時間冇一隻眼捉得住。

嗰一刻我就知我做嘅嘢值幾多。我起咗嗰個裝住呢啲片段嘅檔案庫。搜尋得到、睇得到、真嘅。我做咗啲有用嘅嘢 — 而每一秒我都享受。

我唔知我見到嘅係咪 UFO。但我知，總有一日，有人會坐低，攞住呢啲紀錄，一格一格咁睇。個檔案庫會喺度，因為我起咗佢。

我學到啲咩

日日出貨，唔係就停滯。嗰兩個月嘅沉默教我嘅，比衝刺仲多 — 一個冇 momentum 嘅 project，係一個你已經放棄咗嘅 project。每日 deploy 等個循環夠緊：起、睇佢上線、修、重複。

等真 user 拉你向前。

起咗佢，放喺人搵得到嘅地方，當有人敲門嗰陣，聽。

AI pair programming 係力量倍增器，唔係替代品。我留低做 product 個腦 — 每個決定、每句「呢樣真係 work 唔 work」、每個咩先重要嘅判斷。Claude 做對手。判斷力從來冇離開過間房，因為判斷力係我嘅。

之後我將 realufo.org 嘅日常照顧交咗畀 Muse — 監察、維護、嗰啲靜靜哋嘅功夫。我返去起嘢。呢樣就係我做嘅事：我起我用嘅嘢，同埋畀所有人用嘅嘢。
