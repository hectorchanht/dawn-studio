# Dawn Studio Changelog

What shipped, when it shipped. Newest first.

## 2026-11-16 — Dawn Studio: Changelog and Examples pages

This site gains the two pages it was missing: a public changelog, so what shipped is visible instead of buried, and an Examples page that shows each app doing a real job, step by step. The changelog is also available as Markdown and an RSS feed.

## 2026-11-07 — Dawn Studio: Every www and stray subdomain now lands on the main site

App subdomains keep serving their app, while www.<app> and any other extra subdomain 301-redirect to the main Dawn Studio site, keeping the path and query intact.

## 2026-11-05 — RealUFO: Disclosure hub and a homepage that follows the latest release

A new /disclosure hub gathers the key milestones of the official UFO release programme with an FAQ, and the homepage now surfaces the latest release dynamically as new files land in the archive.

## 2026-11-03 — OpenMusic: Smarter queue, bilingual search, original artists first

Tapping a song now keeps your queue by default (or rebuilds the Up Next tail, your choice in Settings). Bilingual titles are searched by their Chinese part, so songs resolve far more often, and automatic playback always prefers the original song by the original artist — never a cover in disguise.

## 2026-10-29 — Dawn Studio: Privacy page and a real 404

dawnstudio.app now has a plain-language privacy page — no accounts, no cookies, cookieless analytics only — and unknown addresses return a proper 404 instead of the homepage.

## 2026-10-25 — RealUFO: New official releases publish themselves

When a new batch of declassified files appears on the official source, it is archived and published on RealUFO automatically, and every figure on the site is computed live from the archive database.

## 2026-10-24 — OpenMusic: Public Supporters wall

Supporters who tip OpenMusic can now appear on a public Supporters wall — or stay hidden; the choice is theirs at verify time. Per-song downloads stay free for everyone.

## 2026-10-23 — Numerology: Academy lessons and FAQ, in three scripts

The numerology site now carries its academy lessons and a plain-language FAQ in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English, with tap-to-read classical references behind the key terms.

## 2026-10-23 — Dawn Studio: ZhConvert joins the shelf; libraries go public on npm

ZhConvert — Simplified ↔ Traditional conversion for text, Markdown, subtitles and JSON — joins the app shelf, and the small libraries extracted from our apps (@dawn-studio/auth, zh-convert, pronounce, ai-cache, drag-scroll, theme) are published on npm for anyone to use.

## 2026-10-20 — ClipBin: ClipBin sends its own mail now

ClipBin's sign-in mail now comes from ClipBin's own domain, the same rule every Dawn Studio app follows: each app speaks with its own voice.
