Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

This site

dawnstudio.app is a static site. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no comments and no forms, and it does not collect personal information from visitors.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages and apps get used. It is cookieless, does not track you across sites, and does not build a profile of you. We see aggregate numbers only.

The apps

Each Dawn Studio app handles data its own way. Most are local-first: your data stays in your browser or on your device unless a feature you use needs a server. Check the app itself for its details.

Questions

Dawn Studio is run by Hector Chan. You can reach him through hectorchan.com.

本網站

dawnstudio.app 是靜態網站，沒有帳戶、登記、留言或表格，亦不會收集訪客的個人資料。
