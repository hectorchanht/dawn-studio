Why build it this way?

Because free apps that people love attract the wrong kind of attention — takedowns, blocks, copycats. A studio home that never moves, plus apps that can move in hours, is how the gifts survive. The maverick part isn't the risk; it's giving the work away anyway, and engineering it so nobody can easily take it away from the people it was given to.

Questions

Do I need an account to listen or watch?

No. On both OpenMusic and OpenMovies you can play for free with no account. An account only stores your personal stuff — liked, playlists, watchlist, history and resume / Continue Watching — and lets it follow you across devices and domain moves.

Why does OpenMovies have no domain of its own?

Deliberately. A free app that will never be monetised doesn't need a domain bill — it launches on a Dawn Studio subdomain, openmovies.dawnstudio.app. If it ever outgrows that, this free port will announce the move first.

Why not just use one domain forever for everything?

We would if we could. Free, no-ads services get blocked and pressured at the domain level — lawsuits and copyright claims included. A permanent gateway under Dawn Studio plus fast moves is how services like BingeBang stayed reachable for years; we're using the same survival playbook.

A site looks exactly like OpenMusic or OpenMovies but has ads. Is it real?

No. Real Dawn apps have no ads, no pop-ups and no redirects. A pixel-perfect copy with ads is still a copycat — leave it, and check the address with the checker above.

Will I lose my library when an address changes?

No — your account and library live with the app, not with the address. Log in on the new official address (verified here) and everything is there.

Is there an official app on the app stores?

Not via any app-store listing claiming to be us right now. If we ship one, it will be announced on this free port first. Anything else using our names is a copycat.

What free app is coming next?

Undecided — that's the fun of it. When it's real, it gets its own tab above, its own official-address card, and an entry in the checker, before it's linked anywhere else.
