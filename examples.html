Music player · Web + Android

Find one song, keep a whole library

You hear a song title in two languages and want the real version, not a cover.

  1. Search onceType the title: bilingual titles are searched by their Chinese part, across Chinese and independent sources at once.

  2. Play the originalThe player picks the original song by the original artist first, and queues the rest without disturbing what you built.

  3. Keep itSave it to your library, then explore the artist's discography or a ready-made year pack.

Result: One search becomes a library you own, and per-song downloads are free.

Script converter · Web

Convert subtitles without breaking a single timestamp

Your video is ready, but the subtitles are in the wrong script and a naive converter would mangle the timing lines.

  1. Drop the fileSRT, VTT, LRC and ASS are all understood, or paste plain text and Markdown instead.

  2. Convert dialogue onlyTimestamps and markup stay byte-identical; only the spoken text changes script.

  3. Download and shipPhrase-level accuracy, including protected terms that must never change.

Result: Subtitles in the other script, timings untouched, ready to publish.
